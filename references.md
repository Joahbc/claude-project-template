# Reference sites

Sites worth studying, with the patterns each is good for. Added at `/retro`, usually from an `/audit --learn` run. Open the linked learnings for detail.

| Site | Type | Good for | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| [studiosobra.com](https://www.studiosobra.com/) | Flower studio, Shopify shop plus installations (Copenhagen) | Photo-led dark shop (ink and bone palette, one typeface at extreme sizes); size-as-product pricing (five named sizes in one switcher); case study as facts plus a quote (venue, date, scope, client quote); enquiry form with chips and budget bands | Studio Sobra audit, 2026-11-10 |
| [brandrupraadgivning.dk](https://brandrupraadgivning.dk/) | One-person fire safety consultancy, bilingual DA/EN brochure site (one.com) | Bottom contact strip with tap-to-call on phones; motion from the trade itself (a floor plan drawn on scroll); white "paper on paper" fields and cards on #FBF8F1; one page per service with a contact box and the other services; company voice with the person's page in third person | Brandrup Rådgivning build, 2026-11-12 |
| [atera.spa](https://atera.spa/) | Luxury spa and bathhouse, Glasgow (WordPress, three.js, Lenis) | **Joah's style.** Warm cream and dark brown, one serif wordmark over full-bleed photography; the hero image grows from a small frame as you scroll; Lenis smooth scroll | Creative pack brainstorm (homepage crawl), 2026-11-12 |
| [loveandmoney.com](https://loveandmoney.com/) | Brand and digital design consultancy (Next.js) | **Joah's style.** One huge word edge to edge ("LAM") on a single hot colour, small caps statement below; warm off-white grid sections with product photography | Creative pack brainstorm (homepage crawl), 2026-11-12 |
| [t-able.org](https://www.t-able.org/) | Taiwanese food culture platform, bilingual ZH/EN (Webflow, GSAP, SplitText, ScrollTrigger) | **Joah's style.** A red line drawn down the page as you scroll, connecting sections; giant red display words; bilingual switch in the header | Creative pack brainstorm (homepage crawl), 2026-11-12 |
| [shelbykay.dev](https://shelbykay.dev/) | Freelance front-end developer portfolio (Lenis) | **Joah's style.** Oat and olive palette; name spread across the full width with a rotated second line; numbered, coded work grid with a project list | Creative pack brainstorm (homepage crawl), 2026-11-12 |
| [synchrodogs.com](https://synchrodogs.com/) | Art and fashion studio (Nuxt, three.js) | **Joah's style.** Saffron and chocolate; wordmark edge to edge; colour-coded section tabs across the top; dense small caps statement under a huge headline | Creative pack brainstorm (homepage crawl), 2026-11-12 |
| [mathiasadolfsson.dev](https://mathiasadolfsson.dev/) | Creative engineer portfolio (Nuxt, three.js) | Liked. Curved WebGL carousel of project videos you drag through; tiny quiet type around it; works on phones | Creative pack brainstorm (homepage crawl), 2026-11-12 |
